\begin{abstract}

This thesis deals with the deployment of an intrusion detection and prevention system (IDS/IPS) to secure the network infrastructure of the Hubert Koutoukou Maga National Teaching Hospital (CNHU-HKM) in Cotonou. An audit carried out during an internship in the hospital's IT and network department showed that the analysed segment has neither visible segmentation nor any intrusion detection mechanism. After a comparative study of the available solutions, the Suricata engine is selected and deployed on the OPNsense firewall platform, first in detection mode and then in prevention mode, with custom rules for DICOM and HL7 flows.
